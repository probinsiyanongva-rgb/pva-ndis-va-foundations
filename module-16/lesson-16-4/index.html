1. Progress Reminder

Your evidence has been collected, selected and labeled. Now assemble the final version so someone else can understand it quickly.

2. Why This Matters

A portfolio is more than a folder of files. The reviewer should be able to see what you practiced, what skills each artifact demonstrates and how the evidence relates to the role.

3. Learn

Use the complete workflow:

Collect → Select → Organize → Label → Explain → Present

Collect

Gather completed course evidence.

Select

Choose the most relevant showcase artifacts.

Organize

Use clear folders and filenames.

Label

Mark fictional training artifacts.

Explain

Add a short description of the skill demonstrated.

Present

Arrange the showcase so the reviewer can understand it quickly.

4. See It

A useful showcase entry can use this format:

Artifact: Invoice Administration Practice
Skill: Administrative checking and discrepancy documentation
Context: Fictional Wattlebird provider simulation
Evidence: Invoice-check record and supporting fictional delivery evidence
Training status: Fictional training artifact

This gives context without overstating the experience.

5. Try It

Complete your final portfolio self-audit.

Check:

Completeness

Are the required evidence folders present?

Accuracy

Do filenames and descriptions match the actual files?

Privacy

Is all practice information fictional and appropriately labeled?

Labeling

Can a reviewer clearly distinguish training from real work?

Consistency

Does your profile match your portfolio?

Relevance

Do your selected showcase pieces relate to the NDIS VA work you are pursuing?

Presentation

Can a reviewer understand the portfolio without a long explanation from you?

Work it here or in your own file. Saved in this browser only as you type.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: What makes a portfolio credible?

Model answer: Accurate evidence, clear organization, relevant examples, honest labeling, consistent descriptions and a clear distinction between training practice and actual client experience.

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

8. Work Boundary

Do not add real participant information to your training portfolio.

Do not present Wattlebird as a real employer or client.

Do not present the course certificate as an official NDIS certification.
