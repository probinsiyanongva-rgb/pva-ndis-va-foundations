Certification Submission Contract

Module 16 completes the portfolio portion of the certification evidence set. It does not create a separate submission route.

When delivered through PVA Academy, submit the complete certification evidence set through the NDIS VA Foundations → Certification Submission route on the course page. The complete set combines the M15 operational assessment evidence with the M16 portfolio and presentation evidence.

Your M16 contribution is:

  1. certification evidence folder structure containing your course evidence;
  2. client showcase / selected portfolio artifacts;
  3. refined VA profile;
  4. cover letter;
  5. outreach message; and
  6. completed portfolio self-audit.

Keep the fictional-training disclaimer and honest representation requirements on all portfolio evidence. Do not present Wattlebird, the course exercises, or the course certificate as real NDIS employment, real client work, or official NDIS certification.

M15 remains the source of the integrated capstone assessment. M16 completes the portfolio and presentation evidence required for the certification review.

Go to NDIS VA Foundations → Certification Submission
