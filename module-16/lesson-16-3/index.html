Lesson 16.3 — Profile, Cover Letter and Outreach

1. Progress Reminder

Your portfolio shows evidence. Your application explains why that evidence is relevant to the role.

2. Why This Matters

A useful application connects your actual skills to the work the client is hiring for. Specific evidence is more useful than broad claims.

3. Learn

Use this application structure:

Role direction → Relevant skills → Evidence → Availability → Concise call to action

Role direction

State the type of NDIS VA work you are pursuing.

Relevant skills

Mention skills connected to the vacancy, such as:

  • administrative accuracy;
  • scheduling;
  • documentation;
  • invoice checking;
  • privacy-aware information handling;
  • follow-up;
  • professional communication;
  • systems/SOP work.

Evidence

Point to specific practice evidence.

Availability

State when and how you can work, using accurate information about yourself.

Call to action

End with a simple next step, such as inviting the client to review the portfolio or discuss the role.

Avoid generic claims such as “expert” when your evidence is training-based.

4. See It

Instead of:

“I am an expert NDIS VA who can handle everything.”

Use a specific training-based statement such as:

“I completed a fictional NDIS provider simulation covering scheduling, invoice checking, privacy-aware administration, follow-up and escalation. My portfolio includes the practice evidence from those workflows.”

The second statement tells the reviewer what you actually practiced.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: Does every claim in your application have honest evidence behind it?

Model answer: Each claim should be supported by either genuine experience or clearly identified training/practice evidence. The wording should make that distinction clear.

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

7. Prove It

Complete the profile, cover letter and outreach message.

Your application materials should be consistent with the portfolio.

Draft or track your Prove It evidence here. Saved in this browser only — save the finished evidence in your own file with the name the lesson gives you.

8. Work Boundary

The course does not certify you as an NDIS professional or represent Wattlebird as a real employer.

Present the course as training and practice.

If you later gain real client experience, describe that experience separately and accurately.
