Lesson 16.1 — Certification Evidence vs Client Showcase

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

Your course evidence and your client-facing showcase serve different purposes.

← Module 16 overview

2. Why This Matters

A certification reviewer may need to see the full body of evidence. A potential client or employer usually needs a quicker view of the skills you can demonstrate.

Keeping these two layers separate makes your portfolio easier to review.

3. Learn

Build two layers.

Layer 1 — Certification Evidence

Keep the complete evidence set from the course.

Use the course evidence structure:

  • 01_Workplace_Foundations
  • 02_NDIS_Admin
  • 03_Operations
  • 04_Privacy_Compliance
  • 05_Capstone
  • 06_Job_Readiness

This layer is your complete training record.

Layer 2 — Client Showcase

Select approximately 5–6 polished artifacts that quickly demonstrate relevant skills.

Suggested categories:

  1. Communication
  2. Organization / operations
  3. Roster / scheduling
  4. Invoice / administrative checking
  5. Privacy / compliance awareness
  6. Capstone / workflow evidence

The showcase is a selection from your evidence. It is not a second course record.

4. See It

A learner might have many completed practice items:

  • a communication rewrite;
  • a handover;
  • roster work;
  • invoice-check evidence;
  • privacy practice;
  • an SOP;
  • capstone records.

The certification folder can retain all of them.

The client showcase might select six pieces that together show a broader range of administrative ability.

5. Try It

Open M16_Portfolio.

Sort your completed evidence into the six certification folders.

Then create a shortlist of 5–6 showcase candidates.

For each candidate, record:

  • artifact name;
  • skill demonstrated;
  • source module;
  • why it belongs in the showcase;
  • fictional-training label confirmed: Yes/No.

Work it here or in your own file. Saved in this browser only as you type.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: Why should the client showcase be smaller than the complete evidence set?

Model answer: The showcase is designed for quick employer/client review. It should communicate the strongest relevant evidence without requiring the reviewer to examine the entire training record.

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

7. Prove It

Complete your portfolio selection.

You should have:

  • a complete certification evidence structure;
  • a 5–6 item showcase shortlist;
  • a reason for each selected artifact.

Draft or track your Prove It evidence here. Saved in this browser only — save the finished evidence in your own file with the name the lesson gives you.

8. Work Boundary

Your portfolio represents your training and capabilities.

It must not imply that Wattlebird was a real employer or client, or that the practice records represent real participant work.

9. Next Step

Next, you will check every selected artifact for honest labeling and representation.
