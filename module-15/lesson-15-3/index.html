Lesson 15.3 — Follow Up, Escalate and Close

2. Why This Matters

An unresolved task can disappear from view if nobody records who owns it, what happens next or when it should be checked again.

3. Learn

For every unresolved capstone item, record:

  1. outstanding item;
  2. owner;
  3. next action;
  4. channel;
  5. follow-up trigger or date;
  6. response/outcome;
  7. closure condition;
  8. status.

Use Follow_Up_Log for the required follow-up evidence.

Closure principle

A message being sent does not automatically mean the task is closed.

Closure happens when the defined closure condition has been met and recorded.

4. See It

A useful follow-up record might read:

Outstanding: Authorized review required.
Owner: Named responsible person.
Next action: Review the documented finding and provide direction.
Channel: Provider-approved channel.
Trigger: Follow up when the agreed response point is reached.
Outcome: Record the response received.
Closure: Record the final action and evidence.

The actual owner and action must come from the capstone scenario, source evidence and provider procedure.

5. Try It

Review your open capstone items.

For each unresolved item, update Follow_Up_Log.

Then check whether the task's closure condition has actually been met.

If not, keep it open.

Work it here or in your own file. Saved in this browser only as you type.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: When can an unresolved task be marked closed?

Model answer: When its defined closure condition has been met and the evidence of completion or authorized outcome has been recorded.

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

7. Prove It

Your follow-up evidence should show:

  • owner;
  • next action;
  • channel;
  • trigger;
  • response/outcome;
  • closure condition;
  • final status.

Any required escalation should remain visible in the record.

Draft or track your Prove It evidence here. Saved in this browser only — save the finished evidence in your own file with the name the lesson gives you.

8. Work Boundary

Follow-up does not transfer decision authority to the VA.

The VA can chase an outstanding administrative response according to provider procedure. The VA does not turn an unanswered request into an approval or make the missing decision personally.

9. Next Step

Once your task records are complete, you will prepare an end-of-week handover and perform a self-audit.
