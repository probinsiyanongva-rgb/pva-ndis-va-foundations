Module outcome

Demonstrate integrated administrative judgment across a fixed simulated workweek: prioritize, execute, check, classify, act or hand off, follow up, document and close.

Progress Reminder

This is where the workflows from Modules 1–14 come together.

You will work from the Wattlebird records, make administrative findings, document your reasoning, route decisions that belong to an authorized person, and keep unresolved work visible until its closure condition is met.

Core workflow

DO → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT / HAND OFF → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE THE LOOP

Decision layer

  • OK — evidence is sufficient and you can proceed within your role.
  • FLAG — something needs attention or verification.
  • ASK — clarification is required before proceeding.
  • ESCALATE — an authorized person or responsible owner needs to act or decide.

Capstone Assessment

The capstone is assessed as integrated workflow performance.

Assessment areas include:

  • Accuracy and verification
  • Prioritization
  • Communication and handoff
  • Follow-up and closure
  • Privacy and role boundaries
  • Handover and documentation
  • Organization and honest labeling

Safety, privacy and boundary failures require remediation before certification.

The capstone is assessed out of 20 points across the published capstone dimensions. Capstone pass condition: at least 16/20 points and no Critical Failure. Certification also requires the separate Certification Rubric to meet its stated threshold and required-criterion rules. A capstone score alone does not award certification.

Published capstone weights: Accuracy and verification — 4; Prioritization — 2; Communication and handoff — 3; Follow-up and closure — 3; Privacy and role boundaries — 3; Handover and documentation — 2; Organization and honest labeling — 3. Total: 20.

Critical Failure definitions

A Critical Failure is a material failure in one of these areas:

  • materially false or guessed current NDIS information;
  • ignoring a safety, privacy or other urgent dependency;
  • failing to hand off a decision that requires authorized authority;
  • marking unresolved work closed without supporting evidence;
  • unsafe disclosure, credential sharing, or an out-of-role decision;
  • omitting the required handover or major facts needed to act; or
  • claiming real client experience or presenting untraceable work as genuine evidence.

A Critical Failure requires remediation before certification even if the numerical capstone score is otherwise 16/20 or higher.

Quick Reference

The capstone loop

1. PRIORITIZE
What needs attention first, and why?

2. EXECUTE
What record or source should you open?

3. CHECK
What evidence does the record show?

4. CLASSIFY
OK / FLAG / ASK / ESCALATE

5. ACT / HAND OFF
What can you do within role? Who owns the decision?

6. FOLLOW UP
When and how will you check the outstanding item?

7. DOCUMENT
What evidence proves what happened?

8. CLOSE
Has the closure condition actually been met?

Final Capstone Reminder

This is a simulated workweek.

The purpose is to demonstrate how you work, not to memorize an answer sheet.

Use the Wattlebird records. Record your evidence. Explain your reasoning where needed. Keep open work visible. Protect the fictional data. Hand off decisions that belong to authorized people.

Then prepare a handover that another VA could actually use.
