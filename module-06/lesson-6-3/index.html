Lesson 6.3 — Management Types and Administrative Implications

The next question is:

Who manages the funding?

← Module 6 overview

2. Why This Matters

Management type can affect who handles payment and financial administration.

The NDIS currently identifies three management options:

  • self-managed;
  • plan-managed;
  • NDIA-managed.

The NDIS also explains that different parts of a plan can be managed differently.

That makes management type an important field to check before an invoice, claim, or payment-related task is routed.

3. Learn

Self-managed

The participant, nominee or representative manages the funding, including paying providers and managing financial records.

Plan-managed

A registered plan manager manages the relevant funding, pays providers and manages financial records.

NDIA-managed

The NDIA manages the funding, including provider payments and financial records. The NDIS also refers to this as Agency-managed.

A useful VA check

Before processing a payment-related task, ask:

  1. What is the participant's management type for the relevant funding?
  2. What does the current provider workflow say?
  3. Where should this document, invoice, or query be routed?
  4. Is anything unclear or conflicting?

The management type helps inform the workflow. It does not give the VA authority to approve funding.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

Liam's fictional plan says:

Plan management: Plan-managed

That is a fact you can record.

The fictional plan also says:

Service agreement: Draft — manager review required

That is another fact.

A VA can track the agreement and route it for manager review.

A VA should not mark the agreement approved simply because the document exists.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: Why should management type be visible during invoice checking?

Model answer:
Because management type can affect who handles payment and financial administration. The VA should verify the correct workflow before routing the invoice.

Question: Does “plan-managed” mean the VA can approve an invoice?

Model answer:
No. It identifies a management arrangement. Approval authority remains with the authorised person or workflow.

8. Work Boundary

Management type is an administrative routing clue.

It is not permission for a VA to:

  • approve funding;
  • override a plan;
  • determine clinical suitability;
  • change management arrangements;
  • approve a disputed invoice.

If the workflow or authority is unclear, ASK or ESCALATE to the appropriate person.
