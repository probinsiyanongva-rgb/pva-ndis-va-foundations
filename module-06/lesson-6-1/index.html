Lesson 6.1 — How to Read a Plan Administratively

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

In Module 5, you practiced recognizing what an NDIS VA can explain and when a question needs to be referred.

Now we move from general NDIS context to a document you may encounter in administrative work: a participant plan or plan-related record.

Your job is to read accurately, identify what is visible, and record what still needs confirmation.

← Module 6 overview

2. Why This Matters

A plan contains information that can affect administrative work.

A VA may need to locate:

  • participant name or ID;
  • plan period;
  • management type;
  • support budget information;
  • category or support information;
  • notes or administrative instructions.

A careful VA separates what the document actually says from what they assume it means.

3. Learn

Use this reading sequence:

IDENTIFY → LOCATE → RECORD → CHECK → ASK

Identify

Confirm that you are working from the correct participant record or document.

Locate

Find the relevant fields.

Record

Capture the information accurately.

Check

Look for missing, conflicting, or unclear information.

Ask

Route questions that require an authorised answer.

For training, the Wattlebird plan is deliberately simplified. A real provider's plan format may differ.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

Suppose you are asked:

“Can you check Liam's plan details before we continue with the support request?”

You locate:

  • participant: Liam Dawson (P13);
  • management type: plan-managed;
  • plan period: 01/10/2026–30/09/2027;
  • Core and Capacity Building allocations;
  • service agreement status: draft — manager review required.

You can record those facts.

You should not turn the information into a funding, clinical, eligibility, or authorisation decision.

For example:

Good administrative note:
“P13 Liam Dawson — plan-managed. Plan period 01/10/2026–30/09/2027. Core and Capacity Building allocations are listed in the fictional training plan. Service agreement is marked draft — manager review required.”

8. Work Boundary

A VA may locate, summarize, organize and route plan information within the provider's authorised workflow.

A VA should not independently decide:

  • eligibility;
  • clinical suitability;
  • whether a support is authorised;
  • whether a disputed support should be funded.
