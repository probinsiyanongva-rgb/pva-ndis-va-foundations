Lesson 9.4 — Roster Prove and Handover

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

The goal is a roster that is accurate and traceable: someone reviewing your work should be able to see what changed, why, who owns unresolved work, and what confirms closure.

← Module 9 overview

2. Why This Matters

A spreadsheet edit without a clear record can leave the next person wondering what happened. A good handover makes the work understandable to someone who was not doing the task with you.

3. Learn

For each selected roster issue, build a simple evidence chain:

Source record → Finding → Classification → Owner → Action → Follow-up → Closure

Follow-up record

Use Follow_Up_Log when the work remains open.

Record, at minimum:

  • task/issue ID;
  • owner;
  • next action;
  • channel;
  • follow-up trigger/date or condition;
  • closure condition;
  • status/notes.

Closure is evidence

Examples of closure evidence include:

  • the authorized owner confirms the decision;
  • the roster reflects the confirmed change;
  • the affected booking has been checked;
  • the required communication has been recorded;
  • no unresolved item remains in your handover.

Sending an email alone is not automatically closure.

4. See It

Handover example

Roster handover — Wattlebird simulation

Completed: Checked selected roster exceptions against staff and inbox records.
Escalated: Double-booking requires the Operations Manager's decision.
Options prepared: Coverage options identified for the unfilled shift; no replacement promised.
Follow-up: Open items are recorded in Follow_Up_Log with owner and closure condition.
Pending: Confirmation of the authorized roster decisions.
Closure check: Recheck affected bookings after decisions are confirmed.

This handover tells the next person what is done and what is still open.

5. Try It

Complete the selected Wattlebird roster tasks using:

  • Roster
  • Staff
  • relevant Inbox items
  • M9_Extended_Practice
  • Follow_Up_Log

For each unresolved item, record the owner, next action, follow-up trigger, and closure condition.

Then prepare a short handover containing:

  1. completed work;
  2. escalated decisions;
  3. options prepared;
  4. pending items;
  5. follow-up owner;
  6. closure checks.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: What makes a roster handover useful?

Model answer: It clearly separates completed work from pending decisions, names the owner of unresolved items, states the next action and follow-up trigger, and explains what evidence will confirm closure.

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

7. Prove It

Your Module 9 Prove pack should contain:

  1. Adjusted/annotated roster evidence — with notes showing what was checked and what was confirmed.
  2. Follow-up/closure evidence — using Follow_Up_Log for unresolved items.
  3. Handover — a concise summary of completed work, pending decisions, owners, and closure checks.

8. Work Boundary

Your Prove work demonstrates administrative accuracy and judgment within role.

You are showing that you can identify an issue, prepare useful information, hand off a decision, follow up, and document closure. You are not demonstrating authority to approve staffing decisions, determine worker eligibility, make employment decisions, or provide clinical decisions.
