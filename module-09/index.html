Module Outcome

By the end of this module, you should be able to manage basic roster administration, identify clashes and coverage issues, document options, escalate decisions, and track follow-up without making an unauthorized provider, employment, clinical, or worker-eligibility decision.

Progress Reminder

You are building a repeatable administrative workflow. Each task should leave evidence of what happened, what remains, and who owns the next action.

Use the course workflow:

DO → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT / HAND OFF → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE THE LOOP

Use the decision layer:

  • OK — evidence is sufficient and you can proceed within your role.
  • FLAG — something needs attention or verification; record it and route it.
  • ASK — clarification is required before proceeding.
  • ESCALATE — an authorized person needs to act or decide.

A roster problem is not automatically solved when you send a message. Keep ownership, follow-up, and closure visible.

Public-Holiday Check

For Australian scheduling, treat a public holiday as a verification point. Check the relevant site/location, current official public-holiday information, and the provider's own operating procedure before confirming a roster change. Record the source or provider instruction used and hand off any decision that requires authorization.
