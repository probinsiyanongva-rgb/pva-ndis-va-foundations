2. Why This Matters

Incident records are part of a provider's operational record. A useful record helps the responsible person understand what happened without having to reconstruct the event from scattered messages.

The VA's role is administrative: capture facts, preserve the record, route the matter and follow up.

3. Learn

The Wattlebird incident workflow is:

RECOGNIZE → RECORD → ESCALATE → FOLLOW PROVIDER PROCEDURE → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE

When recording an incident:

  1. Record what was reported or observed.
  2. Record the date/time information that is actually known.
  3. Identify the participant or people involved from the available record.
  4. Record immediate actions already taken.
  5. Separate facts from assumptions.
  6. Route the matter to the responsible owner.
  7. Record the handoff and follow-up.
  8. Keep the matter open until the provider's closure condition is met.

Facts vs assumptions

Fact:

Farah reported that Hannah slipped in the kitchen and hit her head.

Fact:

Farah said she called an ambulance and was at the hospital.

Assumption:

Hannah definitely has a concussion.

The last statement is a medical conclusion that the source does not provide. Do not add it.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

Open Inbox and search for E23 — “Hannah had a fall.”

The message was received Sat 7 Nov, 2:15 pm. Farah says Hannah slipped in the kitchen during the morning shift, hit her head, and that an ambulance was called.

Notice the two different time facts:

  • the event is described only as happening “this morning”;
  • the email was received at 2:15 pm.

Do not invent the event time.

For this training scenario, the appropriate response is ESCALATE to the key personnel / incident owner.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: Can you write “Hannah suffered a concussion” because she hit her head?

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

Model Answer & Reasoning

No. The source confirms a head impact and ambulance response, but it does not provide a diagnosis. Record the reported facts and leave medical assessment to the appropriate people.

Question: What should you do with an urgent incident?

Model Answer & Reasoning

Record the known facts and use the provider's urgent procedure immediately. The authorized provider person determines the appropriate incident/reporting response.

8. Work Boundary

The VA does not independently:

  • diagnose an incident;
  • investigate its legal status;
  • decide whether an event is legally reportable;
  • submit a report to the NDIS Commission unless the provider explicitly assigns that authority;
  • decide medical action.

The VA records facts and follows the provider's procedure.
