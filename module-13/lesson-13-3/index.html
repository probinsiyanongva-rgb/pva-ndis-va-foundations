Lesson 13.3 — Complaints and Screening Follow-Up

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

Incident records focus on events. Complaint records focus on concerns raised by participants or others. Screening records focus on a worker-status dependency.

Both require neutral documentation and clear ownership.

← Module 13 overview

2. Why This Matters

A complaint record can become part of an ongoing resolution process. A screening issue can affect a roster or work assignment. In both cases, incomplete notes can make the next person's job harder.

3. Learn

A. Complaints

The NDIS Commission describes a complaint management and resolution system as processes for identifying, assessing, recording, managing and resolving complaints. It also emphasizes keeping people informed about progress and decisions.

For the VA, a useful complaint record captures:

  • what the person said;
  • when it was raised;
  • who or what is affected;
  • relevant examples or dates, if available;
  • immediate action taken;
  • owner;
  • next action;
  • follow-up trigger;
  • closure condition.

Keep the language neutral.

Complaint:

“My worker arrived 45 minutes late three times this month.”

Avoid converting it into a conclusion such as:

“The worker is unreliable.”

The second statement is an interpretation.

B. Worker screening

Current NDIS Commission guidance says registered providers are responsible for identifying risk-assessed roles and ensuring required workers have appropriate screening clearance. The Commission's worker-screening system records clearance status, expiry and eligibility information.

For this course, the VA may record a screening status or expiry flag and route it to the authorized screening/operations owner.

The VA does not decide eligibility to work from a spreadsheet alone.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: Is “Samuel's complaint is valid” an appropriate VA conclusion?

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

Model Answer & Reasoning

No. The VA records what Samuel reported and routes the complaint through the provider's complaint process. The responsible owner handles assessment and resolution.

Question: Can the VA decide that an expired screening record means the worker is legally prohibited from every type of work?

Model Answer & Reasoning

No. The VA records the status and routes it to the authorized screening/operations owner. The provider's authorized process determines the applicable work and screening action.

8. Work Boundary

The VA does not independently:

  • decide whether a complaint is justified;
  • choose a complaint remedy;
  • determine legal worker eligibility;
  • override a roster because of a screening flag;
  • alter screening records without authorization.
