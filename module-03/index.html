Module Progress Reminder

In Modules 1 and 2, you learned to understand the work environment and communicate clearly.

Now we add another part of the VA workflow:

ORGANIZE → CHECK → PRIORITIZE → ACT / HAND OFF → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE THE LOOP

The decision layer remains:

  • OK — proceed or complete because the required checks are satisfied.
  • FLAG — an exception needs attention or verification through the normal owner/workflow.
  • ASK — factual clarification is needed.
  • ESCALATE — authority, urgent action, or an issue involving safety, privacy, or compliance is beyond your role.

This module uses free tools and fictional practice data.
