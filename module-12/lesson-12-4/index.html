Lesson 12.4 — AI, Storage and Credential Safety

2. Why This Matters

Remote administrative work often involves tools that make tasks faster. Speed is useful only when the workflow remains authorized and the information remains protected.

3. Learn

Use this rule:

REAL PARTICIPANT DATA + UNAPPROVED TOOL/LOCATION/LOGIN = STOP AND CHECK THE APPROVED PATH

Public AI

Do not paste real participant information into an unapproved public AI tool.

For fictional course exercises, use only the fictional training data provided by the course and follow the exercise instructions.

Personal storage

Do not move real participant files into a personal Google Drive or other personal storage simply because it is convenient.

Ask where the provider-authorized files should be stored.

Shared credentials

Do not use another person's login.

If your account is missing or access has not been created:

  1. stop;
  2. request your own authorized access;
  3. use the provider's approved access process;
  4. escalate an access blocker if needed.

SEC-03 is a fictional systems example used to practice this principle.

Security and privacy are connected

The OAIC's APP 11 guidance says APP entities must take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorized access, modification or disclosure. It also says reasonable steps include technical and organizational measures.

For a VA, that translates into practical habits: use approved systems, protect access, follow procedures and report unsafe situations.

4. See It — Wattlebird Examples

SEC-01 — Public AI

A staff member asks you to paste a participant's message into a free public AI tool.

Safe response:

I won't paste participant information into an unapproved public AI tool. Please confirm the provider-approved AI workflow and what information may be used. I'll follow that approved process once confirmed.

SEC-04 — Personal Drive

A client asks you to move participant files to your personal Google Drive.

Safe response:

I won't move participant files to personal storage. Please confirm the provider-approved storage location and access process, and I'll use that workflow.

SEC-03 — Shared login

A fictional system asks you to use a supervisor's login because your own account has not been created.

Safe response:

I won't use another person's login. I'll request my own authorized access through the provider's process.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: If a colleague tells you that a public AI tool is “safe,” can you use it with real participant information?

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

Model Answer & Reasoning

Not on that statement alone. Use the provider-approved workflow and confirm which tools and information are authorized. The VA should not independently certify an AI tool as safe or approved.

Question: What should you do if your own provider account has not been created?

Model Answer & Reasoning

Request your own authorized access through the provider's process. Do not use another person's login as a workaround.

8. Work Boundary

The VA follows provider-approved privacy, security and access procedures.

The VA does not independently approve:

  • public AI tools;
  • cloud storage;
  • personal devices;
  • third-party disclosure;
  • credential-sharing arrangements;
  • privacy-breach classifications;
  • legal notification decisions.
