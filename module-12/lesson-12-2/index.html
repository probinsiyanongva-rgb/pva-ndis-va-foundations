1. Progress Reminder

You now have a simple way to reduce unnecessary information handling. Remote work adds another question:

Is this workflow authorized for this information?

← Module 12 overview

2. Why This Matters

A remote VA may work from another country, use cloud services and access provider systems from a personal workspace. Those circumstances make provider-approved systems, permissions and procedures especially important.

3. Learn

For this course, use:

STOP → CHECK POLICY → USE APPROVED WORKFLOW → ASK/ESCALATE IF UNCLEAR

The OAIC's APP 8 guidance says that before an APP entity discloses personal information to an overseas recipient, it must take reasonable steps in the circumstances to ensure the recipient does not breach the APPs, subject to exceptions. The OAIC also states that the entity can remain accountable for certain overseas handling.

This is regulatory context. It is not a substitute for the provider's own privacy and security procedure.

For a VA, the practical rule is:

  • follow the provider's approved systems;
  • use only the access assigned to you;
  • do not create a personal workaround for convenience;
  • ask before using a new storage, transfer or processing method;
  • do not assume that being offshore automatically makes a workflow either allowed or prohibited;
  • escalate when the provider's authorization is unclear.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

A client asks:

“Can you download these participant files to your personal Google Drive? It will be faster.”

The safe response is not to move the files first and ask later.

The Wattlebird practice response is:

  1. Do not move the files.
  2. State that you need the provider-approved storage/access path.
  3. Ask the appropriate manager or IT/privacy owner.
  4. Record the request if it remains an open dependency.
  5. Follow the approved workflow once confirmed.

This is a fictional training procedure, not a universal provider policy.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: What should you do when you are unsure whether an offshore workflow is authorized?

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

Model Answer & Reasoning

Pause the handling that is uncertain and ask for the provider-approved workflow. If the issue requires authorization or creates a privacy/security risk, escalate through the provider's procedure. Do not invent a workaround.

Question: Does “offshore” by itself tell you whether a workflow is permitted?

Model Answer & Reasoning

No. The actual workflow, provider authorization, applicable privacy requirements and approved systems matter. The VA should verify the approved path.

8. Work Boundary

The VA follows provider policy and approved systems. The VA does not independently certify a cloud service, AI tool, device, country or storage method as privacy-compliant.
