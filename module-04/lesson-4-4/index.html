2. Why This Matters

A handover is more than a summary.

A useful handover lets another person continue the work without reconstructing the entire task.

For Wattlebird, a practical handover records:

  • what happened;
  • current status;
  • owner;
  • next action;
  • channel;
  • follow-up trigger;
  • closure condition;
  • whether the affected person was informed.

Your availability statement should state the agreed working window and time zone.

For Wattlebird:

Monday–Friday, 8:30 am–12:30 pm Melbourne time.

For the simulation week:

Monday–Friday, 5:30 am–9:30 am PHT.

If you are writing an availability statement for an actual client, use your actual agreed hours. Do not copy the Wattlebird hours into a real client agreement.

4. See It

Worked handover example — E02

E02 reports that Sophie appears rostered for two overlapping shifts:

  • Tom: 9:00 am–1:00 pm Melbourne
  • Jack: 12:00 pm–4:00 pm Melbourne

The VA can identify the overlap.

The VA should not decide which participant's shift Sophie should work.

A useful handover could read:

Task: E02 — possible overlapping shifts\ What happened: Sophie reported that her roster shows Tom 9:00 am–1:00 pm and Jack 12:00 pm–4:00 pm on Monday 2 November.\ Current status: Potential roster conflict identified; coverage decision not yet made.\ Owner: Operations Manager, Rhonda Blake.\ Next action: Confirm which shift Sophie is assigned to and arrange any required coverage.\ Channel: Wattlebird internal work channel / approved operations communication channel.\ Follow-up trigger: Before the affected shifts begin.\ Closure condition: Roster is corrected or confirmed and affected worker(s) are informed.\ Requester informed: Sophie has already reported the issue; update her after Operations confirms the action.

Notice what the handover does not do: it does not choose the shift for Operations.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question

What makes a handover operationally useful?

Model answer

It gives the next person enough factual context to continue the task: what happened, what is complete, what remains, who owns the next action, when to follow up, and what will count as closure.

Question

Why is "I told Rhonda" not enough?

Model answer

Because it does not show what was handed over, what Rhonda needs to do, when the task should be checked again, or what evidence will show that the issue is closed.

8. Work Boundary

A VA can:

  • check times and dates;
  • verify location-specific information;
  • document availability;
  • prepare a handover;
  • track follow-up;
  • confirm closure evidence.

A VA should route decisions that require provider authority, such as changing participant services, approving exceptions, choosing coverage, applying charges, or changing an agreed work arrangement.

The purpose of a handover is to preserve continuity and ownership.
