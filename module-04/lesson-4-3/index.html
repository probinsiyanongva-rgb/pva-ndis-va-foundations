Lesson 4.3 — Public Holidays Are Location-Specific

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

A date can be correct and still be incomplete.

For scheduling work, you also need to know where the service is happening.

← Module 4 overview

2. Why This Matters

Public holidays are not automatically the same across every Australian state, territory, district or locality.

Wattlebird deliberately gives you:

  • a Melbourne location;
  • a Gold Coast location;
  • the same calendar date.

This creates a practical check before you tell someone that a service is cancelled, moved or unaffected.

3. Learn

For the Wattlebird simulation:

Tuesday 3 November 2026 is Melbourne Cup Day.

Victoria lists Melbourne Cup Day as a public holiday across Victoria, unless an alternate local holiday has been arranged by a non-metropolitan council.

Queensland's official 2026 special-holiday list shows local special holidays on 3 November in particular districts, but Gold Coast is not listed for that date. Gold Coast has its own listed show holiday on 28 August 2026.

Therefore, the course does not treat 3 November 2026 as a Gold Coast public holiday merely because it is a Victorian public holiday.

The important lesson is the method:

location → date → official local source → record the result.

Public-holiday facts should be rechecked each year and when the locality matters.

4. See It

Return to Inbox → E09.

Ben is a participant with a 9:00 am Gold Coast support on 3 November.

His cousin in Melbourne says it is a public holiday.

That statement is relevant information, but it does not answer the scheduling question.

The service location is Gold Coast.

So the administrative check is:

  1. identify the service location;
  2. identify the date;
  3. check the relevant Queensland source;
  4. confirm whether the locality is affected;
  5. record the result;
  6. communicate or route the answer according to Wattlebird procedure.

The learner is checking the source, not independently creating a provider holiday policy.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question

Why is "Australia is on a public holiday" an unsafe shortcut?

Model answer

Because public holidays can be state-, district- or locality-specific. The relevant check depends on the location connected to the task.

Practice conclusion for E09

For this simulation, the learner should verify the Gold Coast location against the Queensland source rather than applying the Victorian Melbourne Cup Day holiday automatically.

The learner should also follow Wattlebird's process for confirming the participant's support rather than independently changing the roster.

8. Work Boundary

You can perform the administrative lookup and document the result.

You can communicate a verified factual result when the provider's procedure allows it.

You should route decisions about cancellations, participant service changes, charges or exceptions to the authorized Wattlebird decision-maker.

Public-holiday information is a source-checking task, not a substitute for the provider's scheduling policy.
