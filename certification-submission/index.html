Submit your complete certification evidence set

The course package does not transmit certification submissions. When delivered through PVA Academy, submit the complete certification evidence set through the NDIS VA Foundations → Certification Submission route on the course page. The package itself does not transmit files.

The M15 capstone is not the entire certification submission. M15 supplies the integrated operational assessment evidence; M16 supplies the portfolio and presentation evidence. Submit the complete set together through the certification route.

How to submit

  1. Put your complete certification evidence set (all 10 items below) in one shared folder — for example, a Google Drive folder.
  2. Set the folder's sharing to "Anyone with the link" (or equivalent). Your reviewer must be able to open it without requesting access.
  3. Open the submission form and choose the course NDIS VA Foundations.
  4. Paste the folder link into Certification Evidence Folder URL (M15 + M16).
  5. After submitting, keep your private status link — it is the only way to check your review.

M15 capstone pass condition

The capstone is assessed out of 20 points across the published capstone dimensions. Capstone pass condition: at least 16/20 points and no Critical Failure. Certification also requires the separate Certification Rubric to meet its stated threshold and required-criterion rules. A capstone score alone does not award certification.

See Module 15 for the published capstone weights and Critical Failure definitions.

Certification Rubric — v2.3

10 criteria × 0–2 = 20 points. Pass: 16/20 or higher, with every Required criterion at least 1.

Pass rule: 16/20 or higher AND every Required criterion scores at least 1.

Remediation: Any 0 on a Required criterion blocks certification until corrected and reassessed. Safety/privacy/boundary failures require remediation.

Calibration: 16/20 is 80%; it demonstrates consistent beginner competence while the required-criterion floor prevents unsafe gaps from being averaged away.

Human review: Final certification is human-reviewed when current-law, privacy, safety, or ambiguous judgment issues arise.
