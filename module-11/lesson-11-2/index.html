2. Why This Matters

A useful invoice check creates a traceable record. Someone taking over the task should be able to understand what you found and what needs to happen next.

3. Learn

Use the complete workflow:

CHECK → CLASSIFY → DOCUMENT → ASK / ESCALATE → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE

CHECK

Review the invoice against the appropriate records.

CLASSIFY

Use the evidence to choose the appropriate course decision:

  • OK — evidence is sufficient for the administrative check.
  • FLAG — an exception needs attention or verification through the normal workflow.
  • ASK — a factual clarification is required.
  • ESCALATE — authority or urgent handling beyond your role is required.

DOCUMENT

Write the evidence, not just the label.

Weak:

FLAG — looks wrong.

Useful:

INV-1011 shows 40 hours. SD-011 shows 4 delivered hours for the same participant, date and item. Route the discrepancy for authorized review.

ASK / ESCALATE

Ask the person who can supply the missing fact. Escalate when the matter requires authority or urgent handling beyond your role.

FOLLOW UP

Track owner, next action, channel and trigger.

CLOSE

Close when the required disposition or handoff is evidenced. A sent message by itself does not establish closure.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

Compare INV-1001 and INV-1004.

INV-1001 has matching fictional evidence. INV-1004 appears to duplicate it: the participant, date, item, quantity and rate match, and the exercise has one delivery record supporting the service.

The correct administrative response is to record the duplicate and route the retention/disposition decision. The VA does not simply delete one invoice because it appears duplicated.

For every non-OK item, write six things:

  1. What did you notice?
  2. Why does it matter?
  3. What will you do next?
  4. Who owns the next decision?
  5. What will you follow up?
  6. What will prove closure?

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: Does FLAG mean “reject the invoice”?

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

Model Answer & Reasoning

FLAG means an exception has been identified and needs attention or verification through the appropriate workflow. The VA documents the evidence and routes the next decision to the responsible owner.

Question: What makes an invoice note useful to the next person?

Model Answer & Reasoning

It identifies the record, states the evidence, explains why the item needs attention, identifies the owner and records the next action or follow-up trigger.

7. Prove It

Before checking the model answers, review every non-OK row using this test:

Can another person understand the issue and take over without asking me what I meant?

8. Work Boundary

Your classification describes the administrative condition you found. It does not replace the authorized person's decision about payment, correction, rejection, dispute or funding.
