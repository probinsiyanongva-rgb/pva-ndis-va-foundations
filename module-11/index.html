Module Outcome

By the end of this module, you will be able to check fictional invoice information systematically, identify discrepancies, document the evidence, route the next decision to the appropriate owner, and follow unresolved items through to closure.

Your M11 Workflow

CHECK → CLASSIFY → DOCUMENT → ASK / ESCALATE → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE

The course decision layer remains:

  • OK — the required checks are satisfied and the work can proceed within role.
  • FLAG — an exception needs attention or verification through the normal owner/workflow.
  • ASK — a factual clarification is needed before you can safely proceed.
  • ESCALATE — the matter requires authority or urgent action beyond your role, including safety, privacy or compliance concerns.

Classification and priority are separate decisions. An item can be time-sensitive while remaining a FLAG or ASK, depending on what the evidence shows.

Real-World Source Boundary

Real NDIS payment and pricing work is current-source work. The NDIS currently states that provider payment requests require information such as participant name/reference, support dates, support item reference and support item price. The NDIS also explains that payment arrangements differ by management type and that providers must make sure claim details are accurate and aligned with applicable funding and pricing requirements.

The current NDIS pricing page states that the 2026–27 pricing schedules set maximum prices and are effective from 24 September 2026; those maximum prices apply to NDIA-managed and plan-managed participants.

The NDIS Support Catalogue identifies support items, claim types and appropriate maximum-price information and is intended to be used with the pricing schedule.

For real provider work, verify the current official source and follow the provider's approved workflow. This course uses fictional prices so that you can practice the administrative process without treating exercise figures as live NDIS rates.
