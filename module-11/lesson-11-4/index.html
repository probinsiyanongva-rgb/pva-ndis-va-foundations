2. Why This Matters

Invoice work often involves another person. A good administrative record continues beyond the first message and shows ownership, timing and closure.

3. Learn

For every unresolved invoice issue, your Follow_Up_Log entry should answer:

  • Owner: Who must act?
  • Next Action: What needs to happen?
  • Channel: Where will the follow-up happen?
  • Follow-Up Date / Trigger: When or after what event will you check again?
  • Response / Outcome: What did the owner actually confirm?
  • Closure Condition: What evidence will show the item is resolved?
  • Status: Open, pending or closed according to the workflow.

Worked Example

Use INV-1015 as the practice example.

The participant ID P99 cannot be verified. The follow-up should identify Rhonda Blake / Operations as the owner, request identity/source confirmation, and remain open until the identity and invoice disposition are recorded.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

Open Invoice_Batch and find INV-1017.

The agreement is Not started and management type is To be confirmed. The invoice therefore has an onboarding dependency.

A useful follow-up identifies Rhonda Blake as the Operations owner, asks whether services should have started, records the trigger for checking again, and keeps the item open until the owner disposition and required next step are recorded.

A message marked “sent” is evidence of communication. Closure requires evidence that the required disposition or handoff has occurred.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question 1: What proves closure?

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

Model Answer & Reasoning

Closure is supported by evidence that the required owner/action has been completed or that the matter has been formally handed off according to the workflow. A note saying “sent” by itself does not prove resolution.

Question 2: If you send a question to finance and receive no response, can you close the invoice issue?

Model Answer & Reasoning

The dependency remains open. Record the follow-up trigger and continue tracking it according to the provider workflow.

Question 3: What should a strong invoice evidence record allow another person to do?

Model Answer & Reasoning

Another person should be able to understand the invoice, see the evidence checked, identify the classification, know who owns the next action, and continue the follow-up without reconstructing the work from scratch.

7. Prove It — M11 Evidence Pack

Complete and retain these pieces of evidence:

  1. M11_Invoice_Check_Log — your completed invoice checks.
  2. Exception notes — evidence-based notes for non-OK items.
  3. Routing evidence — owner/route for each item requiring attention.
  4. Follow_Up_Log entries — unresolved items with next action and trigger.
  5. Closure evidence — where an owner response or disposition is available.
  6. One learning correction — identify one response you changed after comparing your work with the model answer and explain what evidence changed your decision.

8. Work Boundary

Invoice checking is an administrative control. Your evidence should support the authorized decision-maker and preserve an accurate record. Authority for approval, rejection, submission, dispute, pricing negotiation, funding decisions and other provider-level decisions follows the provider's documented workflow.
