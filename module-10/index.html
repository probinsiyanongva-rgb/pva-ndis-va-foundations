Module Outcome

By the end of this module, you should be able to track a participant onboarding workflow, identify missing or unclear information, prepare an administrative service-agreement draft for review, document dependencies, follow up with the right owner, and close the loop without independently deciding eligibility, funding, clinical needs, consent/authority, or provider terms.

Progress Reminder

You are building a repeatable administrative workflow.

Use:

DO → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT / HAND OFF → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE THE LOOP

And classify findings as:

  • OK — evidence is sufficient and you can proceed within your role.
  • FLAG — something needs attention or verification.
  • ASK — clarification is needed before proceeding.
  • ESCALATE — an authorized person needs to act or decide.

A FLAG is not automatically closed. Keep the owner, next action, follow-up trigger and closure condition visible.

Onboarding workflow

Agreement workflow

Decision layer
