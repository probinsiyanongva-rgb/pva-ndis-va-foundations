Lesson 10.4 — Follow-Up and Closure

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

A good administrative record tells the next person what remains and what needs to happen.

← Module 10 overview

2. Why This Matters

An onboarding checklist can look complete while unresolved dependencies are sitting elsewhere.

The Wattlebird workflow expects you to track:

Owner → Next Action → Channel → Follow-Up Trigger → Closure Condition

3. Learn

Return to:

  • M10_Onboarding
  • M10_Agreement
  • Follow_Up_Log

Work through unresolved items one by one.

For each item, ask:

  1. What exactly is unresolved?
  2. Who owns the next decision or action?
  3. What should happen next?
  4. How will the follow-up happen?
  5. When should it be checked?
  6. What evidence will allow the item to close?

A useful closure rule

Sending a message is an action.

It is not automatically closure.

Closure requires evidence that the required outcome occurred or that the item was formally handed over according to the provider's process.

4. See It

Example:

Issue: Service agreement draft has an unconfirmed term.

Classification: FLAG

Owner: Manager / authorized agreement owner

Next action: Review the marked term and return the approved instruction.

Channel: Wattlebird's approved internal communication channel.

Follow-up trigger: Agreed review date.

Closure condition: Authorized review is recorded and the correct agreement version/status is documented.

That is a complete administrative loop.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: When can an onboarding item be considered closed?

Model answer:

When the required outcome has been completed or formally handed over, the evidence is recorded, and the closure condition in the workflow has been met.

An email being sent is not, by itself, proof that the underlying task is complete.

8. Work Boundary

For real work, the provider's authorized systems and procedures control the actual onboarding and agreement process.

As a VA, your evidence should demonstrate:

accurate tracking + clear documentation + appropriate routing + follow-up

rather than independent authority.
