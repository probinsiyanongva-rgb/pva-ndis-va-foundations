Lesson 2.1 — Clear Workplace Messages

1. Progress Reminder

You have already started learning how a VA supports a provider through careful administrative work. Now we turn that work into messages another person can act on.

← Module 2 overview

2. Why This Matters

A workplace message is part of the work record. A useful message helps the recipient understand what happened, what you found, what you need, and what happens next.

3. Learn

A simple workplace message can follow four parts:

  1. Context — What record, task or situation are you referring to?
  2. What you found or did — State the relevant fact.
  3. What is needed — Say what you need from the recipient.
  4. Next step/timing — Explain what you will do after the response.

Compare these two messages:

Weak:

There is a problem with the roster.

Actionable:

Hi Rhonda, I checked the roster for Sophie Lam and found that the 9:00 am–1:00 pm Tom Nguyen shift overlaps with the 12:00 pm–4:00 pm Jack O’Connor shift. I have left both roster entries unchanged. I’m escalating this for your scheduling decision. Please confirm which shift should remain before Tom’s 9:00 am shift begins, and I’ll update the record after your decision.

This example is ESCALATE, not FLAG: the VA has verified the conflict, but an authorized scheduling decision is required. Scenario reference: E02.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

Open the Wattlebird Inbox and look at E02.

Sophie says she is rostered for Tom from 9:00–1:00 and Jack from 12:00–4:00 and asks which shift she should do.

A safe administrative response identifies the overlap, leaves the conflicting entries unchanged, and routes the decision to the operations manager. The VA should not quietly choose which participant loses coverage.

Worked model:

Hi Rhonda, Sophie Lam’s roster shows Tom Nguyen from 9:00 am–1:00 pm and Jack O’Connor from 12:00 pm–4:00 pm on Monday 2 November. The shifts overlap from 12:00 pm–1:00 pm. I’ve left both entries unchanged and am escalating this for your scheduling decision. Please confirm which shift should remain before Tom’s 9:00 am shift begins.

Scenario reference: E02.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question 1: What is missing from “Please check this”?

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

Model Answer & Reasoning

The message does not identify the record, the issue, the requested action, or the owner. A stronger message names the invoice or task, states what was noticed, and says what response is needed.

Question 2: Why is “Which shift should remain?” better than choosing one yourself in the roster-clash example?

Model Answer & Reasoning

The VA can identify and document the conflict. The person responsible for coverage owns the decision about which booking should be retained.

Your messages should be:

  • specific enough to act on;
  • respectful and easy to scan;

8. Work Boundary

The VA communicates facts and routes decisions. The VA should not invent an approval, silently change a roster, promise an outcome, or make a decision that belongs to an authorized person.
