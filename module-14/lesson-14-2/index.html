Lesson 14.2 — Participant and Staff Lookups

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

You have a repeatable navigation method. Now apply it to two different record types while keeping the evidence tied to the actual task.

← Module 14 overview

2. Why This Matters

Administrative work often involves checking information across related records. Accuracy depends on matching the right person, reading the relevant field and recording what the system actually shows.

3. Learn

Use:

  • M14_Participants for participant records.
  • M14_Staff for worker/staff records.

For each lookup:

Find → Match → Read → Record → Classify → Route if needed

A system field is evidence for the workflow. It is not, by itself, permission to make decisions outside your role.

4. See It

SYS-01 asks you to locate P08 and confirm:

  • site;
  • management type;
  • agreement status.

SYS-02 asks you to find W08 and identify the screening status shown in the fictional system, then record the affected roster shift.

For SYS-02, the correct administrative response to an expired fictional status is to route the issue to the authorized screening/operations owner. The VA does not independently decide legal eligibility to work.

5. Try It

Complete:

SYS-01

Find P08 and record the requested fields.

SYS-02

Find W08, record the fictional status shown, identify the affected roster shift, and document the appropriate handoff.

For each task, record:

  • what you found;
  • classification;
  • next action;
  • owner;
  • follow-up trigger.

Work it here or in your own file. Saved in this browser only as you type.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: W08's fictional status appears expired and an affected shift is approaching. What should you do?

Model answer: ESCALATE to the authorized screening/admin owner, identify the affected shift and track the owner's response. Ask who is authorized to verify the status if the ownership path is unclear.

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

7. Prove It

Retain evidence for SYS-01 and SYS-02.

Good evidence separates:

  • the system fact;
  • your administrative action;
  • the authorized owner's decision.

Draft or track your Prove It evidence here. Saved in this browser only — save the finished evidence in your own file with the name the lesson gives you.

8. Work Boundary

The VA may record a displayed status and route the issue.

The VA does not independently decide whether a worker is legally eligible to work.
