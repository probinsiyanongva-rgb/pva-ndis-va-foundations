Module outcome

Progress Reminder

You are building a repeatable administrative workflow. Each task should end with evidence of what happened, what remains, and who owns the next action.

Core workflow

DO → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT / HAND OFF → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE THE LOOP

Decision layer

  • OK — evidence is sufficient and you can proceed within your role.
  • FLAG — something needs attention or verification; record and route it.
  • ASK — clarification is required before proceeding.
  • ESCALATE — an authorized person or responsible owner needs to act or decide.

A FLAG or ESCALATE is not automatically closed. Record the owner, next action, follow-up trigger and closure condition.

Official Source / Current-Information Note

This module teaches generic system-navigation, access-control and administrative workflow skills using fictional systems. It does not teach the learner to reproduce or operate an official NDIS government portal.

Real provider systems, portal names, authentication methods and access processes can change. Before public release or reuse after a material change, recheck the relevant official source and update the review date.

For current NDIS provider-system information, use official NDIS sources such as:

  • NDIS provider information: https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers
  • NDIS provider portal information: https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/working-provider/using-provider-portals
  • NDIS worker screening information: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/workforce/worker-screening

This module's assessment remains focused on transferable administrative skills rather than memorizing a particular portal's appearance.
