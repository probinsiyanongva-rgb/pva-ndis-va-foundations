Lesson 14.3 — Bookings, Claims and Incidents

Practice file: Wattlebird Sandbox Pack v2.3.4 — fictional training data. Work from a copy.Open the Wattlebird practice file

1. Progress Reminder

Real administrative work rarely stays inside one record type. You may move between a booking, a claim and an incident while keeping each finding tied to its source.

← Module 14 overview

2. Why This Matters

Cross-record accuracy matters because one system entry may provide context for another. A good VA can explain where information came from and what still needs an authorized decision.

3. Learn

Use:

  • M14_Bookings
  • M14_Claims
  • M14_Incidents

For each task, use:

Locate → Match → Compare → Record → Classify → Route → Follow Up

The goal is transferable navigation, not memorizing a particular screen layout.

4. See It

SYS-03 — Booking

Find BK-104 and record:

  • participant;
  • worker;
  • date/time;
  • status.

Do not change the booking unless the task and your authorization allow it.

SYS-04 — Claim

Find CLM-203 and compare:

  • item;
  • quantity;
  • rate;
  • participant context.

If there is a mismatch or missing context, document the evidence and route the issue. The VA does not independently approve or submit the claim.

SYS-05 — Incident

Open IR-008 and draft a factual update/handoff.

Record what is actually documented. Do not add a diagnosis, assumed cause or legal conclusion.

5. Try It

Complete:

  • SYS-03
  • SYS-04
  • SYS-05

For every task, record:

  1. source record;
  2. finding;
  3. classification;
  4. next action;
  5. owner;
  6. follow-up trigger;
  7. closure condition.

Work it here or in your own file. Saved in this browser only as you type.

6. Check Yourself + Model Answer

Question: How can another person tell what path you took?

Model answer: Record the system area, record ID, relevant fields checked, finding, action, owner and follow-up. The evidence should let another person understand how the result was reached without having to repeat the entire investigation.

Write your own answer first, then compare. Saved in this browser only.

7. Prove It

Retain your completed evidence for SYS-03, SYS-04 and SYS-05.

A strong result makes the source record and administrative action easy to trace.

Draft or track your Prove It evidence here. Saved in this browser only — save the finished evidence in your own file with the name the lesson gives you.

8. Work Boundary

The VA can identify, document and route an exception.

The VA does not independently:

  • approve claims;
  • submit disputed claims;
  • determine reportability;
  • make clinical decisions;
  • alter records without authorization.
