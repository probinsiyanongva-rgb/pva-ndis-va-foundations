Module Progress Reminder

You are building a repeatable administrative workflow.

For pricing work, the goal is not to remember a table of numbers. The goal is to know where to check, what to record, what evidence to compare, and when to stop and ask or escalate.

Use the course workflow:

DO → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT / HAND OFF → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE THE LOOP

Decision layer:

  • OK — the available evidence supports proceeding.
  • FLAG — something needs attention or verification.
  • ASK — a missing fact or clarification is needed.
  • ESCALATE — someone with the required authority or urgent responsibility needs to act.

A pricing check can move from one classification to another as new evidence becomes available.
