1. Progress Reminder

You can now identify the basic records and perform a pre-action check.

The next skill is deciding what happens after you find something.

Finding an issue is only part of the job.

2. Why This Matters

Consider these four situations:

  • Everything checks out.
  • Something is unusual and needs attention.
  • A fact is missing.
  • The issue belongs with someone who has authority or needs urgent handling.

The next action is different in each case.

That is why we use:

OK / FLAG / ASK / ESCALATE

3. Learn

Classification Meaning Typical next action
OKRequired checks are satisfiedProceed / complete
FLAGException needs attention or verificationRecord and route through the normal workflow
ASKFactual clarification is neededAsk the relevant person and track the response
ESCALATEAuthority, urgent action, or safety/privacy/compliance issue is beyond roleHand off promptly to the appropriate owner and track it

A classification is not the same thing as closure.

For example:

FLAG → route → follow up → confirm resolution → close

An issue remains open until the required next action is completed and the closure condition is satisfied.

4. See It — Wattlebird Example

Imagine a roster entry appears to overlap with another staff member's shift.

The VA checks:

  • participant;
  • staff;
  • date;
  • shift times;
  • related roster records.

The overlap is confirmed.

The VA should not simply delete one shift.

The finding requires a scheduling decision.

A suitable classification is:

ESCALATE

The next action is to hand the conflict to the appropriate scheduling owner, provide the evidence, and track the response.

8. Work Boundary

The VA's administrative judgment includes recognizing when something can proceed and when it needs another person.

It does not include inventing authority.

When the owner or decision-maker is unclear:

ASK.

When the issue requires authority, or involves an urgent safety, privacy, or compliance matter:

ESCALATE.

When an item has been handed off, keep track of it until the workflow reaches its defined closure condition.
